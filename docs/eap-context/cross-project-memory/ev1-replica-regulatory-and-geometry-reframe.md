Two corrections to any feasibility analysis of the EV1 replica (2026-07-16 owner input):

**(a) NC regulatory path is NOT a binding blocker.** The owner has already researched North Carolina Special Construction titling. The state inspection is primarily anti-theft (verifying the vehicle is not stolen); the safety bar is low, and the owner plans to EXCEED it with documentation — receipts, photos, unrequired engineering — held on hand. Insurance for a one-off high-voltage EV remains an open item but was NOT flagged as a stillbirth risk.

**(b) Museum-scan access is NOT a binding gate.** Because the build is from-scratch with no donor, geometry will be RECONSTRUCTED from published references, the service manual, photogrammetry, and LLM-assisted CAD. Scan access is nice-to-have upside only.

**The real near-term critical path** is GEOMETRY RECONSTRUCTION plus the owner leveling up CAD. He has a spacemouse, tried photogrammetry on interior modules (too coarse), and is tracking the state of the art in LLM-assisted CAD / photogrammetry.
