The owner's EV1 replica goal is RESOLVED as of 2026-07-16.

**What it is:** a road-legal DRIVER (not a static display), scratch-built aluminum spaceframe + composite panels. There is NO donor — all EV1s were crushed or are museum-locked, so nothing to restore/convert; everything is built from scratch. Registered in North Carolina under a Special Construction title.

**Budget & timeline:** starts ~$100k (owner's "cheap end" estimate). Multi-year, but NOT a full decade. Open-ended — no hard deadline.

**Labor model:** primarily solo, but actively networking with local metal-fab shops (tentative interest so far) and wants to find other replica builders. Owner is an experienced DIY mechanic (engine rebuilds, brakes, paintwork) who is happy to BOTH learn trades to find his limits AND hire pros where it makes sense.

**Shop:** basic garage, no lift, no 240V yet (trivial to add).
