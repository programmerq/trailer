Insurance for the EV1 replica is largely DE-RISKED as of 2026-07-16.

The owner called Hagerty (a specialty/collector-car insurer that covers kit cars, classics, and special builds). Hagerty's main objection is using a specialty car as a DAILY DRIVER — which does not apply here:

- The owner works from home.
- The replica will be a garage queen kept under a cover, driven ~5k miles/year at most, primarily to car shows — squarely within Hagerty's model.
- His Civic remains the practical primary car; his wife drives an EV6.

Status: not a blocker. A formal underwriter quote against the assembled build documentation would be strong external validation.
